What is OCR Technology?

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a technology that converts images containing text into machine-readable digital text. OCR is essential for digitizing documents, archiving old papers, automating data entry, and making scanned content searchable and editable. Our tool uses Tesseract, one of the most accurate open-source OCR engines available.

Common Use Cases

  • Digitizing printed documents and books
  • Extracting text from screenshots and photos
  • Converting handwritten notes to digital format
  • Automating data entry from forms and receipts
  • Making archived documents searchable
  • Creating digital copies for backup and archival
